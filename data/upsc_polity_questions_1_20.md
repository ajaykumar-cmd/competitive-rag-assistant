# UPSC / State PSC Polity Questions 1 to 20

## Question 1
Chairman of the Constitution's Drafting Committee

**Answer:** Dr B. R. Ambedkar

## Question 2
Date the Constitution came into force

**Answer:** 26 January 1950

## Question 3
Article for equality before law

**Answer:** Article 14

## Question 4
Article for freedom of speech and expression

**Answer:** Article 19

## Question 5
Article for protection of life and personal liberty

**Answer:** Article 21

## Question 6
Article for Right to Constitutional Remedies

**Answer:** Article 32

## Question 7
Article listing Fundamental Duties

**Answer:** Article 51A

## Question 8
Article for National Emergency

**Answer:** Article 352

## Question 9
Article for President's Rule in a state

**Answer:** Article 356

## Question 10
Minimum age to contest Lok Sabha election

**Answer:** 25 years

## Question 11
Minimum age to be a Rajya Sabha member

**Answer:** 30 years

## Question 12
Term of a Rajya Sabha member

**Answer:** 6 years

## Question 13
Ex-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha

**Answer:** Vice President of India

## Question 14
House where a Money Bill is introduced

**Answer:** Lok Sabha

## Question 15
Article for joint sitting of Parliament

**Answer:** Article 108

## Question 16
Article for the Finance Commission

**Answer:** Article 280

## Question 17
Who appoints the Governor of a state

**Answer:** The President of India

## Question 18
Number of Fundamental Rights at present

**Answer:** Six

## Question 19
73rd Constitutional Amendment deals with

**Answer:** Panchayati Raj

## Question 20
Case that laid down the Basic Structure doctrine

**Answer:** Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973)
